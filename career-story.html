From customer conversations to running operations

My career across customer service, technical support, account management, and BPO operations.

By Rhegiree Aquino · Professional journal

My name is Rhegiree Aquino. My background brings together customer support, technical troubleshooting, account management, executive assistance, and team leadership. Across these roles, the work has always depended on clear communication, accurate records, and reliable follow-through.

Starting with the customer

My earlier roles included seasonal Amazon customer service at Sutherland in 2019, Comcast technical support at Startek, and order entry and customer service at Eastwest BPO. I handled customer questions, equipment setup, troubleshooting, orders, and CRM documentation.

At Cloudstaff, I supported Arrivia customers through calls, email, and chat for travel reservations, membership inquiries, complaints, and transactions. That customer-facing experience became the foundation for my leadership work.

Moving into team leadership

As a Key Performance Leader for Arrivia from June 2023 to May 2024, I supervised agents handling cruise quotations and bookings. My responsibilities included monitoring sales conversion, revenue, CSAT, and adherence, alongside coaching and training. I trained more than 50 agents on quoting workflows, pricing standards, and system navigation.

Working across accounts and suppliers

My executive assistant and supplier success work for Avetta involved supplier coordination, pricing validation, quotations, CRM records, and invoicing coordination. Later, as an Account Manager for Bartercard AU, I managed a portfolio of more than 400 Australian SME clients and supported account follow-ups, transactions, and quotations in Syncore.

I also provided technical support for WYBOT robotic pool cleaners, guiding customers through charging, navigation, suction, and device usage concerns, as well as warranty and replacement requests.

Taking ownership of operations

At CloudClerks BPO, my Operations Manager responsibilities covered staffing, scheduling, attendance, performance reporting, client coordination, and payroll preparation. I worked with team leaders, trainers, HR, and support teams to resolve service-delivery concerns and improve internal workflows.

What I bring to my next role

I am looking for opportunities where I can contribute through customer service, technical support, administration, account support, or operations. My training background also includes truck dispatching and freight. My goal is to bring my existing support and coordination experience into a team where I can keep learning and take responsibility for the work.
