Turning attendance and payroll needs into clear workflows

The operational requirements I helped shape for CloudClerks HRIS and the RFL employee portal.

By Rhegiree Aquino · Professional journal

Business tools work best when their rules reflect how people actually work. My involvement in the CloudClerks HRIS and RFL Portal focused on defining operational requirements, reviewing workflows, and identifying issues that needed resolution.

CloudClerks: attendance through payroll

The HRIS requirements covered attendance records, timekeeping disputes, approval rules, locking, separate weekly payroll drafts by team, and payslip presentation. These steps needed a clear relationship so that attendance review could inform payroll preparation.

My focus included how disputes should be reviewed, when records should be locked, and how separate teams should appear in payroll drafts. I also reviewed how gross pay and deductions were presented so that payroll reviewers and employees could understand the computation.

RFL: employee and administrative access

The RFL Portal requirements brought together project administration, attendance, payroll submission and review, employee payslip access, and account controls. Different users needed appropriate access to their own tasks and records.

I helped shape administrative controls, payroll review and locking requirements, and the rule that employee payslip visibility should depend on payment status.

My contribution

My contribution was operational: identifying user needs, defining rules, reviewing behavior, and following up on issues. These projects demonstrate my ability to translate a business process into specific requirements and coordinate improvements to internal tools.

This write-up describes workflows without exposing employee records, payroll amounts, or client account access.
